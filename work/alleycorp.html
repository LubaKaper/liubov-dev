The problem

A venture firm’s network is its most valuable asset, and it mostly lives in people’s heads. When a portfolio company needs an intro, the question is always the same: who do we actually know there, and how well?

We worked directly with AlleyCorp’s partners to turn that question into a product.

Warmth you can explain

Every relationship gets a tier: Hot, Warm, Stale or Cold. The tier comes from specific signals, and each one can be shown to the person looking at it:

  • Co-investment history
  • LinkedIn connections
  • Event attendance, pulled from event platforms like Luma and Swoogo
  • Public press

Signals fade over time. Meeting someone at an event two years ago counts for less than co-investing with them last quarter. A partner can always see why a relationship is rated the way it is. That was the point.

The rating itself never comes from a model. AI helps fill in the picture and makes it easy to search, but the answer to “how well do we know them” is always traceable.

My part

I owned the database schema, the backend enrichment and the MCP server, which lets AI assistants query the relationship data directly. Access is authenticated only, through deep links, with no anonymous access. A daily digest brings the most useful changes to partners without them having to open anything.
