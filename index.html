About.

I came to software from nutrition. I still work the way I learned there: start with the person, figure out what they actually need, then build the thing that fits.

I’m originally from Russia and have lived in New York since 2005, and I speak English and Russian. I studied nutritional science, managed recipe and ingredient data at Plated, and then changed careers through Pursuit.

At Twitter I worked on the Tweet Foundation team, on code every feature team in the iOS app depended on, and led a big modularization milestone there.

I’m comfortable owning a product end to end and just as comfortable as part of a team. At Pursuit I lead project teams that change every few weeks, so I’m used to getting new people moving quickly.

A lot of my work starts vague: a process nobody has written down, a problem the client can’t quite name yet, requirements that shift after the first demo. I’m at home there. I also care about what comes after, the structure and rules that keep a system reliable once people depend on it. That’s what I learned at Twitter.
